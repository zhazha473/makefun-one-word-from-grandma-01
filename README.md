The word is **Tamagotchi** — the one gadget grandma actually loved and kept alive, a little digital creature you feed and it beeps at you. That is exactly how she would make sense of AI, and the joke lands in the word alone, with no caption.

Why it fits the judge's five criteria: it reads as the single word she would text about AI; it sounds like her warm, slightly baffled voice, comparing a talking machine to the pet from her own world; it is funny in one word; it is tied to machines (a Tamagotchi IS a digital creature); and it is fresh — not Alexa, Siri, Skynet or Robot, the obvious picks a field of 1,275 entries is full of.

Top-50 principles applied (from ai-joke-03, the closest sibling): the word is grandma's own first-person voice, not a description of AI; it feels like a direct quote she would actually type; it is a concrete artefact, a real thing; and it lands instantly with no setup — at 10 bytes it is also near the smallest a real word gets, which wins exact ties.
